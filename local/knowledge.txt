Photosynthesis is the process by which plants, algae, and some bacteria use
light energy to convert carbon dioxide and water into chemical energy.
A neural network is a computational model made from connected parameters.
A language model estimates probabilities over sequences of tokens.
A transformer is a neural network architecture that uses attention to mix
information across a sequence.
Lumina-1 is a learning project for local language models, memory, tools,
retrieval, evaluation, and controlled learning.
