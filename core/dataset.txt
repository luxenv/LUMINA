Lumina-1 is a local language experiment.
Lumina learns from examples of text and predicts what character comes next.
The project is built step by step so that every part can be understood.
Language models transform context into probabilities.
Attention helps a model connect information across a sequence.
A useful assistant can combine language, memory, knowledge, and tools.
Lumina is the beginning of a journey from mathematics to an offline AI.
